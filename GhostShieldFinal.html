    About this demo

    The checkout is a mock page with fictional brands. GhostShield starts on its own when the page loads, reads the options and terms, decides, then edits the page. With "Shield live" the reasoning step is a real Shield call (when available). Otherwise the built-in rules take over and the trace says so. The agent never ticks "I agree" or pays for you. Scores are indicators, not verified probabilities of fraud. To scan real websites, GhostShield must be packaged as a browser extension.
